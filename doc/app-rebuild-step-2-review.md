## Review the workflow

1. **Polish a Group 1 clip.** Switch to Polish, edit its title with Edit title or R, adjust timing on the waveform, then choose Ready for Publish or 2. Confirm it leaves Group 1 and appears in Publish's Ready list. This checks that the stage follows the keeper workflow. On a narrow window, finishing the last keeper should return to the list.
2. **Compare and expand the trim.** In Publish, try As is, Trim edges, and Trim all. The As is reference is a play-only render of the selected clip. The export candidate is the single editable waveform: it shows the original recording timeline, shades the audio the trim removes, and pans or extends only within 5 s of the clip. Click outside the clip to hear that nearby original audio (before the clip, playback stops at the clip start), then drag a start/end handle to include it, or drag the background to select a new range. Editing one boundary preserves the other, keeps the view at the same recording time and height scale, and regenerates the rendered preview. The candidate plays the filtered output, with seeking and its playhead mapped through removed pauses. Compare shaded removed regions with rendered playback, especially an internal pause. On a very short or quiet clip, a trim that removes everything should show an alert above the candidate with a Use As is button. Listen to both players and the resulting MP3 to judge whether the cut sounds right.
3. **Work through several exports.** Change the export title and thresholds, export, and confirm the next ready clip retains your mode and thresholds. Open Exported; select a saved clip and confirm its settings match the saved snapshot. Reveal file should open the latest saved output in Finder.
4. **Edit an exported clip.** Nudge its timing, or edit its review title in Polish's Exported/All view. Its saved file should stay accessible, with Exported · changed shown. Changing a render option in its export panel should also explain that re-export is needed. Re-export, check that the flag clears, and confirm the previous file still exists. This verifies the export snapshot rather than relying on the Exported status alone.
5. **Reopen the recording.** Check the edited title, bounds, export path, and changed/matching state. Exports saved by older builds without snapshots must say unverified; re-exporting one should establish a match.
6. **Use a physical controller.** In Settings → controller mapping, bind Export, a mode, reference playback, and your preferred trim controls. In Publish, test replay, reference comparison, export, navigation, and selected-boundary nudges. Explicit export bindings take precedence if they share a control with review actions. Open a dialog and switch stages while holding a control; releasing it should be required before an action fires in the new context. This remains an owner check because automated tests use a simulated controller.
